One hotkey

Three actions, one shortcut you already know.

  1. Select text in any app

    A word, a sentence, a whole draft. In an email, a chat, a document or a web page.

  2. Hold Ctrl, tap C twice

    A small switcher opens at your cursor, already on the action you ran last. To repeat it, just let go.

  3. Tap C to choose, let go to run

    Rewrite opens the popup with your presets. Lookup and Translate answer in a window of their own and never touch your text.

Insights from your rewrites

BrushUp reads back through your edits and shows the mistakes you make most, with a tip for each.

Prepositions of time Frequent
You often use "in" with days and dates, where English uses "on".
Before
See you in Monday.
After
See you on Monday.
Before
The report is due in 3 May.
After
The report is due on 3 May.
Use "on" for days and dates, "in" for months and years, "at" for clock times.

Privacy

Your writing stays close.

Your text goes straight to the AI model.

The app sends the text you select directly to the language model, through OpenRouter. BrushUp's own server never receives it. It only checks your license and gives the app its access key.

Your history stays on your PC.

Rewrites, lookups, translations, flashcards and insights are stored locally. Delete any entry, or clear it all, whenever you like.

Nothing is read until you ask.

BrushUp reads your selection only when you press Ctrl C C, and replaces text only when you press Enter in the Rewrite popup.
